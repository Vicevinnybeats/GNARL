FORMANT MOVING.

Not the cutoff sweeping. A twelve-type zero-delay filter bank with a vowel space you can drive at audio rate, so the movement reads as a voice rather than as a filter.

  • TWO WAVETABLE / GRAINTABLE OSCILLATORS
  • SIXTEEN-VOICE UNISON, PER-VOICE DETUNE
  • 2× AND 4× OVERSAMPLING ON THE VOICE SECTION
  • −65.6 dBc ALIASING, MEASURED

A BILINEAR BIQUAD
CANNOT DO THIS.

Recalculated per sample it is not stable, so a filter built from one cannot be modulated at audio rate — and audio-rate filter modulation is most of what makes a growl. Every filter here is topology-preserving, and a test sweeps cutoff every single sample across the full range to keep that honest.

  • TWELVE FILTER TYPES, ALL ZERO-DELAY
  • FORMANT BANK WITH A DRIVABLE VOWEL PAD
  • PER-CHANNEL STATE, SO STEREO IS TWO PATHS
  • BLOCK-SIZE INVARIANT, ASSERTED IN TESTS
