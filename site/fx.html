FOURTEEN EFFECTS.
ANY ORDER.

Order matters and the rack respects it. Two distortions with an EQ between them sound different from the same two with an EQ after — because the EQ changes what the second curve is given.

  • OTT, DISTORTION ×2, EQ ×2, FILTER ×2
  • CHORUS, FLANGER, PHASER, HYPER
  • DIMENSION, DELAY, REVERB, LIMITER
  • 2× OVERSAMPLED WHEN ANYTHING NONLINEAR IS ON

OFF MEANS
UNTOUCHED.

A mid/side round trip is not bit-exact, so an effect turned fully down still altered every sample, forever. With fourteen in a chain that most patches do not use, every one of them early-outs at zero rather than multiplying by it.

  • EACH SLOT REORDERABLE BY DRAG
  • WET, DRIVE AND MIX PER SLOT
  • DRIVE COMPENSATED SO IT IS NOT A VOLUME KNOB
  • BIT-EXACT BLOCK-SIZE INVARIANCE, ASSERTED
